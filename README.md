# wasteNot

wasteNot is a community-driven platform for sharing items you no longer need. The concept dates back more than a decade and is akin to Buy Nothing or Everything Free groups: it keeps usable goods out of landfills by connecting neighbors who have extra with those who could use a hand.

Unlike other free groups, givers on wasteNot earn tokens whenever they pass items along. Think of these tokens as a karma score&mdash;or even a cryptocurrency reward such as Pi&mdash;that recognizes generosity without turning the exchange into a sale. Everything remains free.

## Why Another Group?

Existing communities are valuable, but wasteNot offers improvements:

- **Rewarding Good Deeds**: Our token system publicly thanks those who give most often.
- **Searchable Listings**: Tag and categorize items so others can quickly find what they need.
- **Optional Delivery**: Earn tokens for helping transport bulky goods to neighbors.
- **Mobile Friendly**: A simple site that works on any phone or computer, no walled garden required.

## How It Works

1. **Sign Up** &ndash; Create a basic profile. No fees or complicated setup.
2. **Offer an Item** &ndash; Post photos and details. Mark whether you're able to deliver.
3. **Receive Tokens** &ndash; When someone claims your item, you earn tokens as a thank-you.
4. **Redeem or Donate** &ndash; Spend tokens on items you need or donate them to local drives through the platform.

Items are always free, and tokens simply provide positive reinforcement for giving. Join wasteNot and help your community reduce waste while sharing what you don't need.
